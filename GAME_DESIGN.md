# AFTERLIGHT: THE LAST GROUND

A realistic 3D open-world survival adventure for Android and web.

## Core concept

Civilization has collapsed. During the day, survivors explore abandoned places, search for supplies, craft equipment and build shelter. At night, strange creatures emerge and the world becomes more dangerous. The long-term goal is to survive, rebuild and uncover what caused the darkness.

## World

The first playable region will connect three environments:

1. **The Fallen City** — abandoned buildings, vehicles, supplies and places to explore.
2. **The Silent Forest** — wildlife, hidden shelters and dangerous creatures.
3. **The Deadlands** — a harsh wasteland, abandoned military facilities and rare resources.

The initial prototype will use a small section of one environment. The world can expand after the core gameplay works.

## Player

- Customizable male or female survivor.
- Starts in ordinary, worn everyday clothing.
- Small, nearly empty backpack.
- Survival clothing, masks and equipment can be unlocked through progression.

## Planned gameplay

- Single-player first; multiplayer is a later milestone.
- First-person and third-person camera options.
- Walking, running and jumping.
- Day/night cycle.
- Exploration, scavenging and inventory.
- Crafting and shelter building.
- Creatures and combat.

## Visual direction

Realistic 3D environments, believable character animation, atmospheric lighting, weather and sound. Graphics must be scalable so the game can run on supported Android devices.

## Development approach

Use Godot 4 as the game engine and GitHub for version control and project backup. Build and test a small playable prototype before expanding the world or adding multiplayer.

## First milestone

Create a small 3D test environment with a controllable survivor, basic movement and a camera that can switch between first-person and third-person.
